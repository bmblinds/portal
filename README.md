# B&M Dealer Ordering Portal 倍美窗帘订购系统

The online ordering system for **B&M Blinds Pro** (B&M Interior Decoration LLC), a manufacturer
of custom window coverings based in Irvine, California. 定制窗帘制造商的经销商订购与报价系统。

## What it does

- **Dealers 经销商** — browse the product catalog, configure custom window coverings
  (sizes in mm or inches, opacity, drive type, colors), see live pricing, and submit
  wholesale orders with quote proposals.
- **Customers 客户** — browse the showroom, track orders from quote to delivery,
  and message B&M directly. Orders are placed in person with our team.
- **Sales team 销售** — build quotes on-site with automatic engine pricing, manage
  their own customers and follow-ups, and hand orders through B&M approval.
- **B&M 工厂端** — confirm orders, track production stages, issue quotations,
  invoices, and factory purchase orders (bilingual PDF documents), manage payments,
  dealers, applications, and reporting.

## Features

- Full pricing engine for shades, blinds, shutters, and drapery, verified against
  real projects to the cent
- Quote workspace: drafts, templates, clone, edit history, and approvals
- Four-stage invoices following the 50% deposit / 50% balance payment flow
- Purchase orders with per-line supplier splitting and production-adjusted dimensions
- Bilingual interface (English + 中文) with optional Español, light/dark themes
- Realtime updates, mobile layout, role-based access control
